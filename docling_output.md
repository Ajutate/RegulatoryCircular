## 1. Welcome &amp; Overview

Welcome to First National Bank! You are joining a team of over 12,000 professionals dedicated to helping individuals, families, and businesses achieve their financial goals. This onboarding guide is your primary reference for the first 90 days of your employment and beyond. Please read it carefully and keep it accessible throughout your onboarding period. ## 3. Code of Conduct &amp; Ethics

All First National Bank employees are expected to maintain the highest ethical standards. The Code of Conduct is not optional -violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be reported to regulatory authorities.

## 3.1  Prohibited Activities

- Accepting gifts, meals, or entertainment from customers or vendors valued over $25 without manager approval.
- Disclosing customer account information to unauthorized persons, including family members.
- Accessing customer accounts for which you are not directly responsible (unauthorized account access).
- Conducting personal financial transactions using bank systems during business hours.
- Using bank resources (computers, phones, email) for personal business, political activities, or illegal purposes.
- Falsifying any bank record, document, or report.
- Engaging in any transaction with a customer in which you have an undisclosed personal interest (self-dealing).
- Discussing confidential bank strategies, financial results, or customer information on social media.

## 3.2  Conflict of Interest

You must disclose any situation where your personal interests could conflict -or appear to conflict -with the interests of the bank or its customers. This includes: romantic relationships with direct reports, ownership stakes in vendor companies, and providing banking services to close family members. Disclose all potential conflicts using the online COI Disclosure Form within 10 days of joining.

## 3.3  Whistleblower Protection

First National Bank strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who, in good faith, reports a suspected violation of law, regulation, or bank policy. Reports can be made anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-ETHIC or at ethics.fnb-internal.com.

## 4. Know Your Customer (KYC) &amp; Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

As a bank employee, you play a critical role in protecting the financial system from being used for money laundering, terrorism financing, and fraud. Failure to follow KYC/AML procedures can result in severe penalties for the bank and for you personally, including criminal prosecution.

## 4.1  Customer Identification Program (CIP)

Before opening any new account or establishing a new customer relationship, you MUST collect and verify:

- Full legal name (as on government-issued ID)
- Date of birth
- Residential address (not P.O. Box for individual customers)
- Tax Identification Number (Social Security Number for US persons, or EIN for businesses)
- Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)

Business customers additionally require: Certificate of Incorporation or equivalent, Beneficial Ownership Certification (for entities with ≥25% ownership), and Business License.

## 4.2  Suspicious Activity Recognition

Report any of the following patterns to your BSA Officer immediately:

- Customers who are unusually secretive about the source of funds or the nature of their business.
- Large cash transactions (≥$10,000) that appear inconsistent with the customer's profile.
- Frequent just-below-threshold transactions (structuring), e.g., multiple $9,900 deposits.
- Rapid movement of large sums through multiple accounts with no apparent business reason.
- Customers who request that records not be kept, or who show unusual interest in reporting thresholds.
- Wire transfers to/from high-risk jurisdictions flagged by OFAC or FATF.
- New customers who immediately request large wire transfers or cashier's checks.

## 4.3  Currency Transaction Reports (CTR) &amp; Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR)

A Currency Transaction Report (CTR) must be filed with FinCEN for all cash transactions exceeding $10,000 in a single business day -regardless of whether the activity seems suspicious. This is MANDATORY and cannot be waived. CTRs must be filed within 15 calendar days.

A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed within 30 calendar days of detecting suspicious activity. Employees must NEVER tip off the customer that a SAR is being or may be filed. Doing so is a federal crime. Contact your BSA Officer for guidance.

## 4.4  OFAC Screening

All new customers and all wire transfer parties must be screened against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list before any transaction is processed. The bank's core banking system performs automated screening, but employees must escalate any potential match immediately. Do NOT process a transaction with a potential OFAC hit -freeze it and contact Compliance.

## 5. Data Privacy &amp; Information Security

## 5.1  Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Obligations

Under GLBA, we are required to protect the security and confidentiality of customers' nonpublic personal information (NPI). NPI includes: account numbers, balances, transaction history, credit scores, Social Security numbers, and any other personally identifiable financial information.

- Never share NPI with third parties without a valid customer authorization or legal basis.
- Always use the bank's secure file transfer system -never email NPI unencrypted.
- Lock your workstation (Win+L) whenever you leave your desk, even briefly.
- Shred all physical documents containing NPI -do not place them in regular waste bins.
- Only access customer data that is necessary for your current job function (minimum necessary principle).

## 5.2  Password &amp; Access Security

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days.
- Never share your password or allow others to use your credentials.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access and privileged systems.
- Report any suspected account compromise immediately to the IT Security helpdesk: ext. 4-HELP.
- Do not install unauthorized software on bank devices.
- Personal USB drives are prohibited on bank computers.

## 5.3  Incident Reporting

Report any actual or suspected security incident -including lost devices, phishing emails, or unauthorized access -within ONE HOUR of discovery to: IT Security Hotline: 1-800555-4357 or security@fnb-internal.com. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate the incident yourself.

## 7. Branch Operations &amp; Daily Procedures

## 7.1  Opening Procedures

7. Arrive at least 15 minutes before branch opens. Sign in on the attendance log.
8. Collect your cash drawer from the vault. Count and verify your starting balance against the Teller Settlement Sheet.
9. Test all equipment: cash counter, coin sorter, ID scanner, workstation login.
10. Review the daily briefing email for rate changes, product promotions, fraud alerts, and operational notices.
11. Ensure your station is clean, organized, and free of personal items visible to customers.
12. Verify the transaction log from the prior day is balanced and filed.

## 7.2  Teller Cash Handling

Cash handling accuracy is critically important. Every teller is responsible for their own drawer. Key rules:

- Count cash in front of the customer at the time of transaction.
- Place bills received from customers face-up and in the same direction before processing.
- Always use the counterfeit detection pen and UV light for bills $20 and above.
- Never leave cash unattended on your counter. Never leave your drawer unlocked.
- For transactions over $3,000, a supervisor override is required.
- Drawer differences must be reported immediately to your supervisor -do not attempt to balance by adjusting records.
- End-of-day balancing must be completed before leaving your workstation.

## 7.3  Vault Access

The vault requires dual control -at least two authorized employees must be present whenever the vault is opened or closed. Never enter the vault alone. Log all vault access in the Vault Activity Register including: time, purpose, employee names, and amounts moved.

## 7.4  Closing Procedures

13. Stop accepting customers 15 minutes before closing time (as per branch policy).
14. Balance your drawer. Complete the Teller Settlement Sheet and have your supervisor sign off.
15. Secure all cash in the vault. Record the closing balance in the Vault Register.
16. Lock all file cabinets. Shred any documents with customer information left on desks.
17. Log out of all systems. Ensure your workstation is locked.
18. Activate the branch alarm. Do not leave alone -at least two employees must exit together (buddy system).

## 8. Customer Service Standards

Every customer interaction is an opportunity to build trust and loyalty. First National Bank measures customer satisfaction through NPS (Net Promoter Score) and monthly branch surveys. Our target NPS is 72+.

## 8.1  The FNB Service Promise (GREET)

- Greet: Acknowledge every customer within 30 seconds of entering the branch. Make eye contact and smile.
- Relate: Use the customer's name at least twice during the interaction. Show genuine interest.
- Explore: Ask open-ended questions to understand the customer's full financial picture.
- Educate: Explain products and services clearly. Avoid jargon. Confirm customer understanding.
- Thank: Thank the customer by name when the transaction is complete. Invite them to return.

## 8.2  Complaint Handling

When a customer expresses dissatisfaction, follow the LAST model:

- Listen: Let the customer speak without interrupting. Do not be defensive.
- Apologize: Acknowledge the customer's frustration sincerely. 'I'm sorry you experienced this.'
- Solve: Offer a concrete resolution. If you cannot resolve it immediately, set a clear follow-up timeline.
- Thank: Thank the customer for bringing the issue to your attention.

All formal complaints must be logged in the Complaints Management System (CMS) within 24 hours. Complaints involving potential regulatory violations (UDAP, discrimination, RESPA) must be escalated to Compliance immediately.

## 9. Compliance &amp; Regulatory Framework

First National Bank operates under a comprehensive regulatory framework. All employees are expected to understand the key regulations affecting their role.

- Bank Secrecy Act (BSA): Requires financial institutions to detect and prevent money laundering. Drives our AML, CTR, and SAR obligations.
- USA PATRIOT Act: Requires Customer Identification Programs (CIP) and enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers.
- Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA): Mandates protection of customer financial data and privacy notices.
- Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA): Prohibits discrimination in lending based on race, sex, national origin, religion, age, or receipt of public assistance.
- Fair Housing Act (FHA): Prohibits discrimination in residential real estate transactions.
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA) / Reg Z: Requires clear disclosure of loan terms, APR, and total cost of credit.
- Truth in Savings Act (TISA) / Reg DD: Requires accurate disclosure of deposit account terms including APY and fees.
- Community Reinvestment Act (CRA): Requires banks to meet the credit needs of all communities they serve, including low- and moderate-income areas.
- OFAC Regulations: Prohibits transactions with sanctioned individuals, entities, and countries.
- Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices (UDAAP): Prohibits practices that mislead or harm consumers. Covers advertising, sales scripts, and fee disclosures.

## 10. Human Resources Policies

## 10.1  Attendance &amp; Punctuality

Regular attendance is essential for branch operations. If you are unable to come to work, notify your supervisor at least ONE HOUR before your scheduled start time by phone -text messages are not acceptable for absence notifications. Excessive absenteeism (more than 3 unplanned absences in a rolling 90-day period) may result in a formal corrective action. Four or more consecutive days of absence require a physician's note upon return.

## 10.2  Dress Code

Customer-facing employees must dress in business professional or business casual attire. FNB logo wear is encouraged on designated days. Clothing with logos, slogans, or graphics (other than FNB branding) is not permitted. Closed-toe shoes are required on the teller line. Visible tattoos and piercings should be minimal and inoffensive in a professional banking environment.

## 10.3  Performance Reviews

Performance is formally reviewed twice per year: mid-year (July) and year-end (December). The review considers: goal achievement, compliance adherence, customer satisfaction scores, team collaboration, and professional development progress. Merit increases are tied to year-end performance ratings on a 1 -5 scale. A rating of 3 or above is required for merit eligibility.

- 401(k): Eligible after 90 days; bank matches 100% of first 4% of salary contributed.

## 11. Emergency &amp; Escalation Procedures

## 11.1  Robbery Response

Your safety is the bank's highest priority. If the branch is being robbed:

19. Stay calm. Do not resist. Comply with the robber's demands.
20. Activate the silent alarm if you can do so safely without being noticed.
21. Note the robber's description: height, weight, clothing, hair color, any distinguishing features.
22. Do not chase the robber. Do not touch the counter area where the robber stood.
23. After the robber leaves, call 911 immediately, then contact your Branch Manager.
24. Lock the branch doors. Do not allow anyone to enter or leave until police arrive.
25. Do not discuss the incident with other employees before speaking with law enforcement.

## 11.2  System Outage

In the event of a core banking system outage, switch to manual backup procedures: collect paper transaction tickets, limit cash transactions to $500 without manager approval, do not process wire transfers, and contact IT Operations at ext. 4-OPS. The Branch Manager must be notified immediately for any outage exceeding 15 minutes.

## 11.3  Suspicious Package / Threat

If you receive a suspicious package, a bomb threat, or discover an unknown device on the premises: Do NOT touch, move, or open it. Evacuate the immediate area. Call 911 and notify your Branch Manager. Follow the building evacuation plan posted at each emergency exit.
